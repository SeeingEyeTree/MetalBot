# 2026-11-04: the sink owns its build power, the nano-col plan, the eco forecast

Uncommitted on `support-groups-arty-tzar` (on top of 022f31f). All new behaviour is opt-in (default off) and on in `candidates/CORE_v4`.

## What changed

**Metal sink** (`bar_framework/metal_sink.lua`, new `bar_framework/sink_lift.lua`)
- `SINK_DECOY_LABS`: some sink spots hold a T2 bot lab (decoy lab) instead of a Dragon lab; a T1 bot con borrowed from the spine is flown there,
  the lab makes 11 decoys for itself, then the sink labs'. `WG.DecoyLabs` (`sink = true`), lab_rules `decoy-lab` want. The line no longer eats lanes
  for decoy labs (`DECOY_LINE = false`) and spine labs no longer make decoys (lab_rules `DECOY_FROM_SPINE = false`).
- `SINK_LIFT`: up to 20 light transports (asked through `WG.LabOrders`, `WG.SinkLiftTids`) fly nano-col nanos to the sink sites: 16 per decoy lab,
  up to 32 per sink lab; they build the lab frame (seconds instead of ~2 min), then guard the lab; given back as decoys arrive (1.5 nanos per decoy);
  only idle nanos (reclaim candidates, fill rows, parked: `SINK_LIFT_TIERS 1`). `SINK_HELPERS 1` (was 6 air cons per frame).
- `SINK_NURSERY = "rows"`: when idle nanos run out, the nano fronts fill the rows the 1010 pattern skipped (also in T1) and refill them after a lift.
- `SINK_CAP_RESERVE 40`: no new decoys / lifted nanos while fewer than 40 + 2 per sink lab unit slots are free (decoys took every slot at the cap).
- `SINK_FORECAST`: once the eco has used 85% of its unit budget, the sink sizes itself for the surplus 150 s ahead (income then - what the eco can
  still spend - army + sink pull now); the first lab may start on it.
- Decoy metal rate from the sink unit (Dragon 12.75, Juggernaut 7.7): `WG.SinkPlan`. Juggernaut sink tested in eco_sim: works, energy-bound.

**Nano-col plan** (new `bar_framework/line_transition/nano_cols/plan.lua`, `fronts.lua`; `LT_CFG.NC_PLAN`)
- One energy target (eco_ledger `EnergyTarget`): the sink's unit once the sink is wanted (`primary`), else army share x spine comp E/M
  (`slot_spine.EnergyPerMetal`) + (1 - share) x 12. `EP_RATIO = false` in CORE_v4 so the energy push uses it too.
- Look-ahead: metal = income + blocks in flight + trend; energy need = target x metal + moho / mex upkeep; next block energy or metal by the gap,
  balanced in-flight mix inside +-5%.
- Skip / come back: energy stalling with metal floating for 20 s stops mex sessions under 60% built (block waits on `f.later`, comes back later).
- Eco forecast: eco spend measured from build progress; remaining work = free budget units x 1300 + retrofit backlog; ledger consumer `eco`.

**Test files**: `stances/legacy/t2_skuttle.lua` + `machines/v4_skuttle_test.lua` (100% Skuttles at T2); `candidates/CORE_v4_0 (was CORE_v4_7f31a42)`,
`candidates/CORE_v4_022f31f` (snapshots of CORE_v4 at those commits, opponents).
