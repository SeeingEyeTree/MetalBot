### defense_crew details
- AA: `corflak` (T2 builders) else `corrl`, 2 + 1 per 400 enemy air value once any enemy air is seen (max 8); alarm raises it to 6-14. Sites
  ring factories/commander at 110-420 elmos, facing the bombers. Anti-nuke sites 250-1100 from an anchor.
- Silo targeting (`Fire`): clusters (650 elmos) of enemy non-mobile unarmed structures, factories and builders (`EI.RaidTargets`); value x1.5 for
  build power / eco / factory; needs >=1500; skips clusters under an enemy anti-nuke and anything within 2500 of our base; one missile per 600 frames.
  `endgame.lua` separately nukes the enemy commander in the won state.
- Loose-site silo building (`SILO_INCOME`) is still in the module but the v16 macro sets it to 1e12 (off).

## Lessons / gotchas
- `CMD.INSERT` at the front of a busy constructor's queue was dropped (the grids' placer rewrites queues). `defense_crew` now orders idle
  constructors directly and appends (`shift`) for busy ones; idle ones are always preferred; an unfinished order is re-sent up to 4 times, then another site/builder.
- The engine logs `Failed to load: <module>.lua (no GetInfo() call)` for every file in `bar_framework` -- harmless.
- Lua files must not start with a BOM (PowerShell `Set-Content -Encoding utf8` adds one and `loadstring` rejects it). Check with lupa.lua51 `loadstring`.
- `Start-Process -ArgumentList` does not expand `%TEMP%`: a relative `%TEMP%\...` folder was created in the repo (removed). Pass full paths.
- `R` in PowerShell is an alias for `Invoke-History`; don't use it as a helper function name.
- Silo capstone grids 6 and 12 are guesses; grid 12 landed at (984, 152) near the map edge and grid 6 at (4344, 136).
