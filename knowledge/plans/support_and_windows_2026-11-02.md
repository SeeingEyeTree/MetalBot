# Notes for later: support groups, value windows, beating DEFENCE_BOT (user, 2026-11-02)

## Home defence (Pounders + Tzars)
- The line of Pounders and Tzars was good, but it stood **behind the important things**. It needs to push out further from the base. More Pounders can be added as the line pushes out.
- The **Tzar is the right unit**; it needs to be in the right spot and kept shooting. Target fire (density + low hp + sticky + focus) is good.
- When defending, Tzars sat **idle a lot**. They should be **air-lifted around to where they are needed** and stay active.
- Air lifts are for getting to the front quickly.
- **What groups the support elements go to needs another look**: which group gets which element, and when.

## DEFENCE_BOT
- CORE_v4 beats CORE_ROWS_ECO well; against DEFENCE_BOT it can be improved.
- If our raids are being stopped by AA and trading poorly, **the plan should change**.
- Lashers can work, but the window where they make sense is too small. The higher-percentage play: **skip unit production for a bit, grow the eco, and build enough fighters**.
- **Plan vs a defensive opponent:**
  1. Grow the eco with map positioning first.
  2. **Contain / hold ~4k out from the enemy base.** Holding 4k out is a win condition and should be cost-effective.
  3. Build a **Basilisk**, rushing it up by air-transporting ~20 nanos to it.
- Flak trucks good, Tzar good.

## Timing and value windows
- **T2 support units come a little late.** If there is space, cut T1 production for a short time, grow the eco, and rush the T2 lab and the T2 units.
- Units get value in **small windows**, and you always have to think about the next step and the window closing:
  - At the start any ground unit gets value. Then air comes online, or porc, or cost-effective holding units, and that window closes.
  - The T2 window: there are not many T2 units yet and still many T1 units for them to kill cost-effectively. Then T3 comes and T2 is less effective.
  - Defending is a little different but the same idea applies: make the good holding units, grow the eco, try to push for space while growing, then go to the next tier.
- In general: get value in a window, grow the eco between windows to be ready for the next one, and be quick about it.

## Conditions
- Base conditions on the **game state**, not hard numbers. Check position and eco where they apply when making a condition (example from this session: the hard-raid threshold became 20% of income x 20 s instead of a flat 400 metal).
