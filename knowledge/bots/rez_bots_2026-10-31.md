# Rez bots, 2026-10-31 (CORE_v4)

User's brief: rez bots reclaim wrecks (no resurrect for now), repair only when there is nothing to reclaim, many of them as the
work needs (max 100), a little aggressive (cheap; reclaim on the enemy side is a 2x swing: we get it, they lose it), always two jobs
queued, buildable from the starter bot lab early, no chasing a moving unit to repair it, only a few units pulled back to heal.
User's verdict after the games: "good enough now ... the other changes are fine tuning".

## How it works (`bar_framework/rez_broker.lua`)
- Jobs, best first: `evade` (90, urgent), `patient` (65, urgent), `wreck` (60), `repair` (50), `follow` (10, idle).
- **Wreck:** best `metal / (distance + DIST_K)` among wrecks (`rez_crew.IsWreck`: `*_dead` / `*_heap` features) that are on our half
  (`OWN_FRAC` 0.5) or within `ARMY_REACH` 1200 of our ground combat units, with room (one bot per `SHARE_METAL` 400 of metal).
- **The one hard rule:** no order (wreck, repair target, move point) within `ENEMY_CLEAR` 500 of a known enemy that can hit ground
  (`c.hitsGnd`; mobile ones only while their position is fresh) or a moving radar dot. Unarmed buildings and air that cannot hit ground
  do not count. The same set drives `evade` (walk away, leaning toward home).
- **Two deep:** the claim carries `queue` (army_broker sends it with shift, `qkey` re-issues when the tail changes); a finished wreck
  promotes the queued one.
- **Repair:** only structures or units slower than `STILL_SPEED`; dropped when it walks out of build range. Units pulled back by
  unit_runtime `HEAL_PULL` (at most 2 + 0.1 x rez bots, <= 6, the most valuable first; held until 90% or 90 s) are the nearest bot's `patient`.
- **Follow:** idle bots go 350 behind the click_army attack group most likely to trade soon (value / (distance to its nearest armed enemy
  or target + 800)), shared out by that score; the stage with no group out. Replaces the old line behind the army centroid.
- **How many:** `Demand()` = allowed wreck metal / 300 + missing army hp / 600 (WG.MetalBot.rez). lab_rules `SQUAD.RezRoom`: max(floor, demand),
  at most one per `REZ_INCOME_PER_BOT` 8 m/s of income, `REZ_MAX` (CORE_v4: 100). Labs: `rez-lab`, `rez-starter` (the starter bot lab between
  the macro's cons, `WG.StarterLab`), `rez-overflow` (T1 bot labs when the room is well past the rez lab's / starter's queue), `rez`.
