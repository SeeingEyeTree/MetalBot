# CORE_v4: air pressure, AA support groups, two T2 air labs per block (2026-10-31)

User request: CORE_v4 vs DEFENCE_BOT (heavy air) lost raid groups to air: too few fighters, too little ground AA.

## What was added (all opt-in; CORE_v4 turns them on)
- **Two T2 air labs per nano-col lab block** (`nano_cols/layout.lua` `SPEC.lab`: dc 72 and 232 in the 320-wide cell, both 8 mod 16). `fronts.lua` counts LABS (`labsPlanned += 2` per block; `NC_T2_LABS_MAX` is in labs, CORE_v4 10).
- **More labs as needed** (`fronts.lua` `LabsWanted`): `NC_T2_LAB_AIR_EXTRA` (+2 while `raidAir.active`, the first lab from `NC_T2_LAB_AIR_INCOME_FRAC` x `NC_T2_LAB_INCOME`) and `NC_T2_LAB_FLOAT_S` (+1 while the metal bank > N s of income). A planned lab is never taken back.
- **Air pressure** (`click_army.lua` `AIR_PRESSURE`, `M.AirPressure()`, published as `mb.raidAir = { active, near, lastF }`, signal `raid_air`): an attacking group is air threatened while the remembered enemy air units that can hit the ground (`EI.AirGroundNear`: Shurikens, Wasps, bombers; not AA-only fighters) within 900 elmos, seen in the last 150 frames, are worth >= max(400, 0.15 x group value). Active while one is and 240 s after. Not kill attribution: a Shuriken stuns, a Wasp may not get the kill. Log `[RAIDAIR] ... ON / off`.
- **AA support element on demand** (`support/aa.lua`, `SUPPORT = { raid = { aa = { when_air = true, n_t1 = 12, n_t2 = 6, min_frac = 0.5 } } }` in CORE_v4's unit_controller): the element is off (n = 0) unless `raidAir.active`; then n = 6 flak trucks once a T2 vehicle plant stands, else 12 Lashers per raid group. Spam / scout / jammer / rez elements stay off.
- **Half a complement**: `army_groups.PoolShort("raid")` + click_army `launchGate` (unit_runtime): while `raidAir.active` the next raid group waits until the pool holds ceil(0.5 n) AA, at most `AA_LAUNCH_WAIT_S` (150 s), then leaves anyway (`[SUP] ... launch held for AA`, `... leaves with a / b`). Reinforcements are not gated.
- **Fighters**: lab_rules `AIR_PRESSURE_FIGHTERS` (CORE_v4 6) added to `FighterWant` while `raidAir.active`.

## Later the same day: forcing the support, fighters forward, what the games showed
- **`support-rush` lab want** (`lab_rules.lua`, prio 148, just under the air rush): while a raid group is held for AA (`mb.supportHold`, published by unit_runtime's `launchGate`), every lab that can make the unit inserts one at position 1 at once (15-frame grace, no pending gate). The passive `group-support` want (prio 96, one insert per lab, then a pending gate and a 60-frame grace) never forced anything.
- **Games with the hold on (3 in parallel, one alone; all vs DEFENCE_BOT, 20 min):** the pool stayed at 0-5 of the needed AA for a minute or more in A, B and C (A: the groups out lacked 7 AA for 30+ s with the rush active), no group ever left on its own AA, 50-100 units piled up at the stage. A and C were cut off by the 750 s wall clock (3 games in parallel are too slow; pass `--duration`), B lost on phi, game D (after the fixes) won with no hold at all. One game each: no verdict on the hold's value.
- **Causes found and fixed:** (1) new AA goes to the groups already out before it reaches the pool, so the hold target is `need + what the groups out lack` (`army_groups.PoolShort` returns the deficit); (2) a Lasher that finished while the base AA screen was short went to the screen, leaving its order "pending" for 3 minutes in `SupportRooms` -- now `army_groups.NoteBypass` counts it as made, and while a hold is on the element takes the unit before the screen; (3) the rush forgets orders no unit answered for 20 s (`SQUAD.SupportHold`); (4) `AA_LAUNCH_WAIT_S` 150 -> 60: after the first burst, Lasher production fell to ~1 per 12 s on a ~200-400 m/s economy, so a longer wait only parks the army. **The fixes are not seen working in a real game** (game D never held a group). The forced test (`AIR_NEAR_MIN = 1`, `min_frac = 1.0`) is the way to see a hold early.
- **Fighters forward** (user: they sat at home and flew across the map): MECH `HOME_FIGHTER_SHARE` 0.25 / `HOME_FIGHTER_MIN` 2 (was 0.5 / 4); click_army `FIGHTER_IDLE_FRAC` 0.5 (no group out: wait half way to the foe, stepped back out of enemy AA, instead of the stage 12% out), `FIGHTER_POST_BACK` 1500 (user: 500 was too close) behind the biggest group, and `FIGHTER_RESPOND`: fighters FIGHT at the centre of an attacking group `AIR_PRESSURE` finds threatened (`g.airThreat`), split over them worst first. Games A, B and C ran with `FIGHTER_POST_BACK` 500 (the user then asked for 1500); game D ran with 1500. Where the fighters stood and how fast they answered air was not measured in any of them.
- **Open:** is the hold worth it at all (min_frac 0.5, 60 s)? Judge it with games where a hold actually happens; raid losses to air before / after were never measured. `deploy.ps1` fails on three stance files that moved to `stances/legacy`.
