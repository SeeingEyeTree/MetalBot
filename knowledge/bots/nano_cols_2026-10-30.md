## Layout (`bar_framework/line_transition/nano_cols/layout.lua`)
B N B N B N B: 3 nano columns, 4 building columns, 1712 elmos wide (`NC_NANO_COLS`).

| | size (elmos) | holds |
|---|---|---|
| building column B | 320 wide | 5 mexes = 4 fusions; 6 winds / 3 AFUS centred (288) |
| nano column N | 144 wide | 3 nanos per 48-tall nano row |
| block | 320 x 192 | 6x4 winds, 5x3 mex / moho, 4x2 fusions, 3x2 AFUS, or a T2 air lab |

Block row r spans a = 192 r +- 96 and holds nano rows 4r .. 4r+3.  Every nano reaches both building columns beside it.
All centres sit on the build grid.  The fronts grow up (+a) and down (-a) from the origin row until the map edge, the
line, the spine or the enemy side, or the eco unit budget.

**Placement** (`fronts.lua` `Candidates`): beyond the line's away edge (the rows' side), columns along the line, origin row
level with the air lab.  If fewer than 3 columns fit there or there are fewer than `NC_MIN_ROOM` (20) rows of room, both
ENDS of the line are tried too (block turned 90 degrees); most columns wins, then most room.  At the default spawns
(2400,1904) the away side has only ~1360 elmos: 2 columns.  With the spawns moved ~400 elmos inward
(`2816,1904;9472,10384`) all 3 fit on the away side.  At the `close` spawns the end-of-line placement gives 3 columns
(eco_sim, 4 seeds, income at 20:00: 1 column on the away side 3.6k, end placement 7.3k, grids 5.5k).

## Who builds what (`fronts.lua`, con_broker jobs `ncnano` 72, `ncbuild` 70, `ncretro` 50, `nchelp` 20)
- **Nano front** (per nano column and direction, T1 air cons): the next nano row by `NC_NANO_PATTERN` "1010" (every other
  row in T1); at most `NC_NANO_AHEAD` (1) block rows ahead of the building fronts beside it; at T2 it goes back and fills
  the skipped rows (`NC_FILL`).
- **Build front** (per building column and direction): the next block.  T1: mex or wind by `NC_E_RATIO_T1` (15) x metal
  income.  Once T2 has started: never wind; an empty "energy gap" where the T2 projection (T1 mex blocks counted as mohos)
  wants energy (`NC_E_RATIO` 15).  T2 air lab blocks: the first at 600 m/s or half the unit budget, then one per 400 m/s,
  4 at most.  At most `NC_BUILD_LEAD` (1) block rows ahead of its nanos.
- **Retro front** (per building column and direction, T2 cons): T1 blocks behind the build front.  Mex -> 15 mohos;
  wind / gap -> fusions (4x2) when energy is short, else 15 mohos; a mex block -> fusions when energy is short and no
  wind / gap is left (`NC_MEX_TO_ENERGY`); wind blocks first once eco units pass 85% of the budget.
- `NC_CONS_PER_FRONT` 3 cons per T1 front, `NC_RETRO_CONS_PER_FRONT` 4; free T1 cons guard nano-row / block builders
  (`NC_HELP_*`, at most 16).  A front with no work frees its cons; inner columns are staffed first.
- **Ferry** (`ferry.lua`, user): a Valkyrie from the air lab carries 2 line nanos into the middle nano column's skipped rows
  next to the origin, and back to the line once 8 nano rows stand (or T2).  `air_transport.lua` does the moves.
- **Unit budget**: `NC_UNIT_FRAC` 0.7 of the cap, counting eco structures only (extractors, energy, storage, nano turrets).
  The sink's labs and decoys must not count: they grew to ~360 units by 20:00.

## Nanos (`nanos.lua`)
- **Focus** (the "interrupts"): every eco frame of the type the economy needs (energy frames while energy binds, mex/moho
  while metal does; T2 labs always) gets up to 6 nanos in reach at `NANO.PRIO.FOCUS` (1.5).  Those nanos are switched to
  HIGH builder priority (`GameCMD.PRIORITY` 34571, confirmed in BAR's `modules/customcommands.lua`) until the frame is done.
- **Parking**: a feedback loop on spend_governor's measured army share.  Army under its share and bank low: park 10% more.
  Over the share or bank floating: unpark.  At least 30% always active.  At 20% it parks only before ~12:00; after that
  metal floats (the army is short of labs, not metal).
- **Reclaim**: at T2, a nano whose reachable blocks are all final is reclaimed by a neighbour (frees cap for more mohos
  further out); 4 stay by each T2 lab.

## Bugs found on the way
- **blueprint_placer `FindFrameAt`** took any frame of the same type within 48 elmos as the item's own.  With items 48
  apart, an item never placed was "handed off" to a neighbour's frame; the session then repaired that finished neighbour
  for ever and held its con.  Fix: opt-in `item.strictFrame` (12 elmos, being built only); the block sets it.  The grids
  have 48-apart winds too and may have the same bug (not changed).
- **IsScoutDef** (lab_rules, unit_runtime): the fast-and-unarmed fallback matched the air transports; it now needs radar
  >= 500 (user).  unit_runtime also leaves transports alone (the army took the ferry's Valkyrie).
